PMS (Property Management System)

This module is an all-in-one property management system (PMS) focused on medium-sized properties for managing every aspect of your property’s daily operations.

You can manage properties with multi-property and multi-company support, including your rooms inventory, reservations, check-in, daily reports, board services, rate and availability plans among other property functionalities.

Installation

This module depends on modules base, mail, sale and multi_pms_properties. Ensure yourself to have all them in your addons list.

Configuration

You will find the hotel settings in PMS Management > Configuration > Properties > Your Property.

This module required additional configuration for company, accounting, invoicing and user privileges.

Derived room type prices

A room type can take its price from another one, e.g. a Superior room costing 10 more than the Double one, so that the pricelists only need the prices of the latter. Set it in the Derived Price section of the room type:

  • Price Derived From: the room type the price is taken from. It must be available in all the properties of the room type, and it can take its price from a third one in turn (e.g. Family = Superior + 20), up to ten levels.
  • Price Variation Type and Price Variation: a fixed amount, in the currency of the pricelist, or a percentage of the base price. Negative values lower it, and the price never goes below zero.
  • Price Rounding: the multiple the prices derived by a percentage are rounded to, e.g. 1 for whole amounts. Zero rounds them to the currency.
  • Derive in Pricelists: the pricelists the price is derived in. Leave it empty to derive it in all of them.

Mind that a derived room type with no rule of its own in a pricelist it does not derive in gets its sales price there.

Working with derived room type prices

The price of a room type is only derived from its base room type on the days a pricelist has no rule of its own for it:

  • An item of the derived room type in the pricelist sets its price on its dates: it overrides the derivation there, and the room type follows its base again once the item is removed.
  • Rules on a product category or on all products cover the room type as well, so they win over the derivation. Keep them out of the pricelists the room types derive in.
  • The base room type is priced by the same pricelist, with its own rules or derived in turn. With no rule at all, its sales price is the base.
  • Pricelists based on another one through category or global rules (e.g. a channel rate 10% above the standard one) apply on top of the derived price. When they hold rules for the base room type only, the derived room type has no rule there either: its price is derived from the price of the base room type in that pricelist, which may not be what you want (e.g. a breakfast surcharge per guest of the base room type). Give those pricelists rules for the derived room types too, or use category or global rules there instead.
  • The base price is computed for the date and the property of the stay only, not for the guests: modules pricing by occupancy apply their rules to the derived room type afterwards.
  • Prices already set on reservations do not follow later changes of the base price: use Update prices on the reservation to recompute them.
  • Massive changes on pricelists leave the derived room types out of the price changes when no room type is selected, so that they keep following their base, except on the dates they have a price of their own, which is kept. Select them explicitly to give them a price of their own.
